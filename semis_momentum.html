Signed Efficiency Ratio — the Trend-Gated Trail (TGT) morning gate
Same net-move ÷ total-distance ratio as above, but SIGNED (keeps the direction of the net move instead of taking its absolute value) — this is what the TGT setup below gates on, decided once at the 30-minute mark (bold column) for SNDK/WDC/MU/TSM only. The unsigned ratio above is direction-blind (only 42% accurate at predicting up-vs-down days, worse than a coin flip) — signing it fixes that specific problem, but signing it has not been shown to produce a profitable trading edge on its own: an initial "12/12 split-half" validation claim was found to rest on a look-ahead backtest bug, and a corrected, properly out-of-sample re-check found no edge (see the TGT card below). Treat this table as a diagnostic of morning direction only, not a proven signal.

Correction (2026-09-05): this setup was originally described here as validated by "12/12 split-half checks" — that claim was wrong. It came from a backtest that bought gated trades at the day's opening price, which isn't achievable since the gate itself can't be confirmed until 30 minutes into the session. Re-run with the real, achievable entry price, those same 12 checks come out 5/12 in the gate's favor — worse than a coin flip. A follow-up grid search (trained only on one separate window, tested out-of-sample on the rest) didn't do better (6/10), and its win/loss pattern was fully explained by which half of the calendar each check fell in, not a real per-day signal. Exit Confidence — profit-taking risk Directional gauge · not a hard trigger
0–100, only shown while a symbol is trading above today's VWAP (i.e. mid up-move) · higher = historically worse odds over the next 10–20 minutes
SymbolScoreRiskWhy
Blends only the two components that actually showed a real backtested edge (VWAP extension relative to today's range, and volume vs. its trailing 20-bar average) — RSI level, MACD deceleration, and momentum deceleration were tested and dropped as noise. 5-day backtest: TRAIN correlation with 10/20-min forward return -0.15 to -0.17, TEST (2 held-out days) -0.26 to -0.31, with the top score quartile historically negative 60-80% of the time over the next 10-20 minutes vs. ~45-50% for the bottom quartile. Use it to decide how much to trim into strength, not whether to hold at all - it doesn't replace the exhaustion exit above, which still drives the actual paper signals.
