⚠️ CORRECTION 2026-09-21 — the daily stop-loss figures above were withdrawn
Everything previously in this box (the “+187–206% range”, the “60–70% effectiveness” estimate, and “out-of-sample validation still holds”) rested on two invalid steps. First, the stop was never modelled by replaying the engine — it was simulated by capping a fixed day_pnl series (stop_amount = 30000 * stop_level, cmd_plan never called), which cannot model a rule change because liquidating positions alters every subsequent day. It also applied a $300 cap (1% of $30,000) to P&L generated by an $80,000 run. Second, the “holdout” was not a holdout: its $36,027 is exactly the Jul 6–Aug 3 slice of the same continuous run, and its “+45%” divides that slice by the original $80,000 even though equity had compounded to $162,679 by then. A genuine fresh holdout returns +11.73%.

Proper engine replay: the daily stop returns +79.71% vs +155.10% baseline over six months and +8.72% vs +11.73% on a real holdout — worse in both windows. It also carries an unfixed double-sell defect; run with that defect left in, the same window prints a fake +1215.80%.

It was never live on real money. The daily stop existed only on a development branch. The production branch that the daily trigger checks out has never contained it — which is also why it never appears in the live run log.
Strategy setup & thresholds
Scaled Dip-Buy / Scaled Peak-Sell · reverse-engineered then parameter-optimized · checked once daily at ~12pm CDT
HUGE_DIP entry
Yesterday's close ≤ -35% cumulative from the trailing 60-day high → deploy 40% of safe settled cash into that symbol.
NORMAL_DIP entry
Yesterday's close ≤ -0.4% vs. the close 2 days ago, and fewer than 5 tranches held since last full exit → tranche-indexed sizing: 95% / 55% / 35% / 20% / 10% of cash for the 1st–5th tranche.
Intraday stop
Live price ≤ -1.51% vs. prior close → sell the entire position. Highest priority, never netted against a same-day buy.
Max hold
Position open ≥ 6 trading days with no STOP/PEAK/GAIN exit of its own → sell entirely. Frees stale capital for fresher dips.
Peak sell
Live price is a new high since the position opened → sell 74.3% of shares, ratchet the peak. May net against a same-day buy.
Gain tiers
Checked only if not a new peak. Gain from entry ≥20% → sell 90%; ≥10% → sell 50%; ≥5% → sell 20%.
Kill-switch + trend gate
Total equity ≥15% below its all-time peak → liquidate everything, block new entries for 20 trading days. Once ever triggered, every future dip candidate must also be above its 50-day SMA, permanently.
Circuit breaker
2+ STOP exits in one run → pause all new entries for that run only.
Position caps
Max 50% of system equity in one symbol. Max 25% of current total equity notional per individual trade.
Universe
Exactly AMD, MU, WDC, SNDK, TSM, INTC, LRCX, STX. Equities only, no options, no leverage — settled cash only.
